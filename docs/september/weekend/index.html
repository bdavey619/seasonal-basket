The weekend meal · September

Chiles rellenos, the plain way.

One meal should feel like the reason you went shopping. In September, it's the one you can only make while the chiles are here.

Use

  • 6 large poblano chiles
  • 8 oz Monterey jack or Oaxaca cheese, cut into batons
  • 1 can whole tomatoes, or 4 ripe tomatoes
  • ½ white onion
  • 1 garlic clove
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Cilantro and lime, to finish

The traditional version is battered in whipped egg and fried. It's worth doing once, and it is not what this is. Roasted, stuffed, and baked gets you most of the way there on a Sunday afternoon.

Do

Char the poblanos on all sides — burner, grill, or dry pan. Steam them covered for fifteen minutes, then slip off the skins, keeping the chiles whole. Cut one slit down each side and pull out the seeds. Blend tomatoes, onion, and garlic; simmer in a little oil with salt until it darkens slightly, about twenty minutes. Tuck cheese into each chile, set them in a baking dish, and bake at 425°F until the cheese gives, about fifteen minutes. Spoon the broth around them, not over them. Cilantro and lime at the table.
