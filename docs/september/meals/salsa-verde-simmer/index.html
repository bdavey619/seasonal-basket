Simmered in salsa verde

A pot of salsa verde and something simmered in it until the sauce thickens. Chicken thighs on Sunday, eggs on Wednesday.

40 minutes for chicken, 15 for eggs · Chicken: dinner and three lunches. Eggs: dinner for one.

1 Pick a protein

  • Boneless chicken thighs 1½ lbs Simmered covered in the salsa, about 25 minutes, then shredded in the sauce.
  • Eggs 4 No meat. Cracked into hollows in the simmering sauce, covered 4–5 minutes.

2 Pick a flavor

  • Green Salsa verde + cilantro A spoonful of salsa verde, cilantro leaves torn over the top.
  • Smoky Charred chile strips + lime Strips of roasted chile, lime squeezed over.
  • Pickled Escabeche + a little brine A spoonful of the jar with its brine. Crunch and acid. How to make the escabeche →

You'll need

  • Salsa verde, a full batch: 1 lb tomatillos, 1 roasted chile, a handful of cilantro, juice of 1 lime, salt
  • Salt

Plus your protein and your flavor.

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. If neededNo salsa verde yet? Boil the husked tomatillos 5 minutes until olive-green, then blend with the chile, cilantro, lime, and salt.
  2. SimmerBring the salsa verde to a simmer in a wide pan with a lid.
  3. 25 minChicken: add the salted thighs, cover, and simmer gently until they shred easily. Shred them right in the sauce.
  4. 4–5 minEggs: make four hollows in the sauce, crack an egg into each, and cover until the whites set.
  5. FinishYour flavor on top.

Leftovers

The chicken keeps four days in its sauce. It's tacos and rice bowls all week.
