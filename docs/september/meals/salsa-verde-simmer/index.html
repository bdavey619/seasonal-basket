Simmered in salsa verde

A pot of salsa verde and something simmered in it until the sauce thickens. Chicken thighs on Sunday, eggs on Wednesday.

Keep

  • A wide pan with a lid
  • Salt

September adds

Start with
  • Salsa verde, a full batch
  • Boneless chicken thighs, or eggs
Choose from
  • Cilantro
  • Limes
  • Monterey jack
  • Black beans

Variations

Chicken thighs · green The default. Simmered 25 minutes, shredded in the sauce.
Chicken thighs · smoky Chile strips stirred in for the last five minutes.
Eggs · green No meat. Eggs cracked into the simmering sauce, covered until set.

Finish

Bring the salsa verde to a simmer in a wide pan. For chicken, add the salted thighs, cover, and simmer gently for about 25 minutes until they shred easily — then shred them right in the sauce. For eggs, make four hollows in the simmering sauce, crack an egg into each, cover, and cook four or five minutes until the whites set.

Works well with

  • Rice
  • Corn tortillas

From Field Notes
