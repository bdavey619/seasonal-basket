Pasta

The green sauce this month is not basil. Roasted poblano, blended with cream, is the most surprising thing you will make in September.

Keep

  • Dried pasta — spaghetti or something short
  • Cream, or Greek yogurt, or a little of both
  • Garlic
  • Salt

September adds

Start with
  • 2–3 roasted, peeled poblano chiles
Choose from
  • Cilantro, torn after the heat
  • Lime
  • Roasted delicata, folded in
  • A spoonful of escabeche on the side

Variations

Poblano + cream + cilantro The base version. Blend the chiles with the cream and a clove of raw garlic until smooth, then warm it — do not boil it.
Poblano + yogurt + lime Lighter and sharper. Take the pan off the heat before the yogurt goes in or it will split.
Poblano + cream + delicata Roasted squash folded in at the end. This one eats like a Sunday.

Finish

Save a mug of pasta water before draining and use it to loosen the sauce in the pan — poblano cream tightens fast. Cilantro and lime go on after the heat is off, never during.

Works well with

  • Chicken thighs, sliced
  • Nothing at all
  • A fried egg on top
  • Cotija, grated over

From Field Notes

  • The parking lot smells like September.

House Flavor
