Sheet-pan dinner

Delicata half-moons and chile strips on one pan, with chicken thighs or black beans. The first fall dinner, while it's still warm out.

Keep

  • A sheet pan
  • Olive oil and salt

September adds

Start with
  • Delicata squash, in half-moons
  • Roasted chile strips
Choose from
  • Boneless chicken thighs
  • Black beans
  • Escabeche
  • Cilantro and lime

Variations

Chicken thighs · smoky Thighs roasted with the squash, chile strips and lime at the end.
Chicken thighs · pickled Escabeche and its brine spooned over the hot pan.
Black beans · smoky No meat. Beans warmed on the pan for the last ten minutes, chile strips, lime.

Finish

Heat the oven to 425°F. Delicata and salted chicken thighs go on the pan with room between the pieces, oiled and salted. Roast 25 to 30 minutes until the squash edges go dark and the chicken is browned. Chile strips go on for the last five minutes.

Works well with

  • Rice
  • Corn tortillas

From Field Notes

  • Don't peel the delicata.

House Flavor

  • Escabeche
