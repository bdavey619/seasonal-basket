Rice bowl

September's rice bowl is built on two things you made once: a tray of roasted chiles and a jar of escabeche.

10 minutes with cooked rice · Dinner or lunch for one

1 Pick a protein

  • Mahi-mahi, leftover A big piece Flaked, cold or barely warmed.
  • Ground beef picadillo, leftover A big spoonful Warmed in a pan, 2–3 minutes.
  • Salsa verde chicken A big spoonful Warmed in its sauce.

2 Pick a flavor

  • Pickled Escabeche + a little brine A spoonful of the jar with its brine. Crunch and acid. How to make the escabeche →
  • Sweet and crisp Apple + lime + cilantro Shredded apple tossed with lime, salt, and cilantro. A slaw in thirty seconds.
  • Green Salsa verde + cilantro A spoonful of salsa verde, cilantro leaves torn over the top.

You'll need

  • About 1½ cups cooked rice
  • Roasted chile strips
  • Roasted delicata half-moons, if you have them
  • Cilantro
  • 1 lime

Plus your protein and your flavor.

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. If neededCook the rice if you need to.
  2. Warm your protein.
  3. Rice, chile strips, delicata, protein.
  4. FinishYour flavor on top.
  5. Lime last, and more cilantro than looks reasonable. If it tastes flat, it's missing the squeeze, not salt.

Leftovers

Pack it as tomorrow's lunch, with the flavor on the side.
