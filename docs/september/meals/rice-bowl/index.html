Rice bowl

September's rice bowl is built on two things you made once: a tray of roasted chiles and a jar of escabeche.

Keep

  • Sticky white rice
  • Protein of choice (chicken, beef, beans, or nothing)

September adds

Start with
  • Roasted chile strips
  • A spoonful of escabeche, with a little brine
Choose from
  • Delicata squash, roasted in half-moons
  • Salsa verde
  • Cilantro, torn
  • Lime

Variations

Chile strips + escabeche + cilantro The version you'll make most often. Three minutes if the jar is in the fridge.
Delicata + chile strips + lime Sweet against smoke. The one that feels like fall showed up.
Salsa verde + beans + cilantro No protein needed. Sharp, green, and finished.

Finish

Lime last, over everything, and more cilantro than looks reasonable. If the bowl tastes flat, it is almost never missing salt — it is missing the squeeze.

Works well with

  • Chicken thighs
  • Ground beef or turkey
  • Black or pinto beans
  • A fried egg

From Field Notes

  • The parking lot smells like September.
  • Don't peel the delicata.

House Flavor

  • Escabeche
