Stuffed roasted chiles

Charred poblanos, stuffed and baked in a simple tomato broth. Picadillo on a weeknight, jack cheese when there's no meat.

Keep

  • A baking dish
  • Olive oil and salt

September adds

Start with
  • 4 roasted, peeled poblanos
  • Picadillo (ground beef, diced apple, chile), or Monterey jack
Choose from
  • Canned whole tomatoes, blended with a little onion and garlic
  • Cilantro and lime

Variations

Ground beef · sweet and crisp Picadillo inside, apple slaw on the side.
Monterey jack · smoky No meat. Cheese inside, chile strips and lime over the broth.
Ground beef · pickled Picadillo, with escabeche at the table.

Finish

For the picadillo, brown the beef hard, then add a little diced onion, one diced apple, a chopped roasted chile, salt, and a splash of the blended tomatoes, and cook until thick. Slit each roasted poblano down one side, pull out the seeds, and stuff it. Spoon the blended tomatoes into a baking dish, set the chiles in it, and bake at 425°F for 15 minutes.

Works well with

  • Rice
  • Black beans
