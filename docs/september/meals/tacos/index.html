Tacos

Chile strips, salsa verde, escabeche, cilantro, lime. Everything on this list came from one shopping trip and two jars.

Keep

  • Corn tortillas
  • Protein of choice
  • Cotija or crumbled feta
  • Lime

September adds

Start with
  • Roasted chile strips
  • Salsa verde
Choose from
  • Escabeche
  • Shredded apple slaw with lime and cilantro
  • Roasted delicata
  • Cilantro, a lot of it

Variations

Chile strips + salsa verde + cilantro + escabeche The September taco. Nothing here is optional.
Delicata + chile strips + cotija + lime The vegetable taco that does not feel like a compromise.
Beans + salsa verde + apple slaw The one to make on a weeknight when nothing is defrosted.

Finish

Warm the tortillas directly over a flame or in a dry pan until they blister in spots, then stack them under a kitchen towel while you set everything else out. Two warm tortillas per taco, not one. That is not excess, it is structural.

Works well with

  • Chicken thighs, simmered in salsa verde and shredded
  • Ground beef or turkey
  • Black beans
  • Whatever came in at the fish counter

From Field Notes

  • The parking lot smells like September.
  • Salsa verde without a recipe.
