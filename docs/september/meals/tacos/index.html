Tacos

Corn tortillas, a protein, and September's jars. The meal this month does best.

15 minutes with cooked protein · Dinner for one, about 6 tacos

1 Pick a protein

  • Mahi-mahi ½ lb Seared 3–4 minutes without moving, flipped, 2–3 more. Flaked in the pan.
  • Chicken thighs, from the salsa verde pot A big spoonful Shredded, warmed in its sauce.
  • Ground beef picadillo, leftover A big spoonful Warmed in a pan, 2–3 minutes.

2 Pick a flavor

  • Green Salsa verde + cilantro A spoonful of salsa verde, cilantro leaves torn over the top.
  • Pickled Escabeche + a little brine A spoonful of the jar with its brine. Crunch and acid. How to make the escabeche →
  • Sweet and crisp Apple + lime + cilantro Shredded apple tossed with lime, salt, and cilantro. A slaw in thirty seconds.

You'll need

  • 12 corn tortillas — two per taco
  • 2 roasted chiles, in strips
  • Cilantro
  • 1 lime
  • Oil and salt

Plus your protein and your flavor.

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. TortillasWarm the tortillas directly over a flame or in a dry pan until they blister in spots. Stack them under a towel.
  2. ProteinCook or warm your protein. Mahi: salt it, pat it dry, sear without moving until it lets go of the pan, then flip.
  3. Two tortillas, protein, chile strips.
  4. FinishYour flavor, cilantro, and lime last.
