The weekend meal · October

Seared rockfish, brown butter, and the October salad.

One meal should feel like the reason you went shopping. In October, it's a fish most people walk past, seared hard in a hot pan and finished in brown butter — the one cooking lesson this month is built around.

Use

  • 1 lb rockfish fillets
  • Half a kabocha squash, cut into wedges
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1 lemon
  • Olive oil and salt
  • 4 handfuls of arugula
  • Seeds of half a pomegranate
  • 4 Medjool dates, pitted and torn
  • A little feta, crumbled
  • Ajvar, for the table

Everything here is on the shopping list. If the counter is out of rockfish, any firm white fillet works the same way.

Do

Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the kabocha wedges with olive oil and salt and roast for 30 to 35 minutes, until the edges are dark and a knife slides through. Meanwhile, salt the fish and leave it on a plate for fifteen minutes, then pat it completely dry. Heat a heavy skillet over medium-high with a thin film of oil until it shimmers. Lay the fillets in, away from you, and don't touch them for three or four minutes, until the edges turn opaque and the fish releases from the pan on its own. Flip, add the butter, and tilt the pan, spooning the foaming butter over the fish for a minute or two until it smells nutty and turns golden brown. Squeeze in half the lemon. Put the arugula, pomegranate, dates, and feta in a bowl and dress it at the table with olive oil, the rest of the lemon, and salt. Serve the fish over the kabocha with the brown butter spooned on top, the salad beside it, and ajvar on the table.
