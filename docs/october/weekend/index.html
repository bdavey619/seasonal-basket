The weekend meal · October

Dutch-oven chicken with delicata and white beans.

One meal should feel like the reason you went shopping. In October, it's the first one that asks for the oven for an hour — and the reason to get the heavy pot back out.

Use

  • 6 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs, about 2½ lbs
  • 1 delicata squash — halved, seeded, cut into 1-inch half-moons, skin on
  • 2 poblanos, seeded and cut into strips
  • 6 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  • 2 cans cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 2 cups chicken stock, or 2 tsp bouillon in 2 cups hot water
  • 1 lemon
  • Olive oil, kosher salt, black pepper
  • Salsa macha, for the table

Any heavy, lidded, oven-safe pot will do if you don't have a Dutch oven. Salt the chicken the night before if you remember; right before is fine if you don't.

Do

Heat the oven to 375°F. Pat the chicken dry and salt both sides. In the Dutch oven over medium-high, with a thin film of oil, lay the thighs skin side down and leave them alone for 8 to 10 minutes, until the skin is deep golden and lets go on its own. Turn them for two minutes, then set them aside and pour off all but two tablespoons of fat. Add the poblano, garlic, and rosemary and stir for three or four minutes. Pour in a splash of stock and scrape up everything stuck to the bottom — that's the flavor. Add the beans, squash, and the rest of the stock, season, and nestle the chicken back in skin side up, with the liquid about halfway up the thighs. Into the oven, lid off, for 45 minutes, until the squash is tender and the meat pulls from the bone. Zest the lemon over the pot and squeeze in half of it. Let it sit ten minutes. Salsa macha at the table. It's better the next day, pulled off the bone and eaten over rice.
