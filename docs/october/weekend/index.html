Dutch-oven chicken with delicata and white beans.

The whole month in one pot, and the braise that teaches you the rest.

Basket Delicata squash, The last poblanos + Protein Bone-in chicken thighs + Pantry White beans, Rosemary, Garlic, Stock, Lemon → Method Braise → In Dutch oven

Use

  • 6 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs, about 2½ lbs
  • 1 delicata squash — halved, seeded, cut into 1-inch half-moons, skin on
  • 2 poblanos, seeded and cut into strips
  • 6 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  • 2 cans cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 2 cups chicken stock, or 2 tsp bouillon in 2 cups hot water
  • 1 lemon
  • Olive oil, kosher salt, black pepper

Salt the chicken the night before, or an hour ahead, if you remember. If you don't, salt it right before. It still works.

Do

About an hour and a quarter, twenty minutes of it active. Serves four, with lunch left over.

  1. Sear

    Heat the oven to 375°F. Pat the chicken dry and salt both sides. Set the Dutch oven over medium-high with a thin film of oil and lay the thighs in skin side down — in two batches if they crowd. Leave them alone for 8 to 10 minutes, until the skin is deep golden and lets go of the pot on its own. Turn them for two minutes, then move them to a plate. Pour off all but about two tablespoons of fat.

  2. Aromatics

    Turn the heat to medium. Add the poblano, garlic, and rosemary to the fat and stir for three or four minutes, until the chile softens and the kitchen smells like it.

  3. Deglaze

    Pour in a splash of the stock and scrape the bottom with a wooden spoon. The brown layer stuck to the pot is the most flavorful thing in it. It belongs in the sauce, not in the sink.

  4. Liquid

    Add the beans, the squash, and the rest of the stock. Season with salt and pepper. Nestle the chicken back in, skin side up. The liquid should come about halfway up the thighs, with the skin above it.

  5. Slow cook

    Into the oven, lid off, for 45 minutes. Below the surface everything turns to stew; above it, the skin crisps again. It's done when the squash is tender and the meat pulls from the bone.

  6. Brighten

    Zest the lemon over the pot, then squeeze in half of it. Taste the beans and salt them if they need it. Let it sit ten minutes. Serve from the pot, with bread for the liquid.

The next day

Pull the chicken off the bone and stir it back into the beans. Warm it with a splash of water, or eat it at room temperature over rice or toast with the other half of the lemon. It's better on Monday.
