The drink · October

Normal Heights Date Shake

One drink. It's still hot, and the dates just came in.

Keep

  • Cold milk
  • Vanilla ice cream
  • Ice
  • Flaky salt

October adds

  • 5 new-crop Medjool dates, pitted

Method

Tear the dates into pieces and let them sit in half a cup of the milk for ten minutes to soften. Blend with another half cup of milk, two big scoops of vanilla ice cream, a few ice cubes, and a pinch of salt until it's thick and nearly smooth — a few flecks of date are the point. Drink it with a straw, outside, on the hottest afternoon of the week. The desert has been making these for a hundred years.

Try another way

Add a shot of cold espresso If it's the afternoon and you need it to count.
Greek yogurt and a frozen banana instead of ice cream If it's breakfast.
A spoonful of tahini If you want it less sweet and more interesting.
Oat milk Thicker than you'd expect, and good.
