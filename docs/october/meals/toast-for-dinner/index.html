Toast for dinner

Thick sourdough, toasted hard in olive oil, with something good on top. Five minutes, and it still counts as dinner.

Keep

  • Sourdough, thick slices
  • Olive oil and salt

October adds

Start with
  • Arugula
  • Something from the week: an egg, the lamb, or the fish
Choose from
  • Ajvar
  • Salted Greek yogurt
  • Pomegranate seeds
  • Lemon
  • Crumbled feta

Variations

Smoky: ajvar + fried egg + arugula The default. Jar spread thick, egg on top, yolk running.
Lamb: yogurt + leftover lamb + pomegranate Warm the lamb in the pan first. Yogurt goes down before it.
Fish: flaked rockfish + arugula + lemon Cold leftover fish, olive oil, lemon, salt. Lunch, too.

Finish

Toast the bread in a skillet with a slick of olive oil, pressing it down, until it's golden and crisp on both sides — much better than a toaster. Build it while the bread is hot. Two slices is dinner.

Works well with

  • A second egg
  • Arugula dressed with lemon

House Flavor

  • Ajvar
