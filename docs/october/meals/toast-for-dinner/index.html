Toast for dinner

Thick sourdough, crisped in olive oil, with something good on top. Ten minutes, and it still counts as dinner.

10 minutes · Dinner for one

1 Pick a protein

  • Fried eggs 2 Cooked in the same pan after the toast, about 3 minutes.
  • Ground lamb, leftover A big spoonful Warmed in the pan, 2 minutes.
  • Rockfish, leftover A few pieces Flaked cold. Nothing to cook.

2 Pick a flavor

  • Smoky Ajvar + lemon A spoonful of the jar and its oil, lemon squeezed over everything. How to make the ajvar →
  • Cool and sharp Salted yogurt + pomegranate Yogurt on the plate first, the hot food on top, seeds over it all.
  • Bright and hot Chile-garlic oil + lime Warm olive oil with a grated garlic clove and chile flakes for one minute. Pour it over; squeeze lime hard.

You'll need

  • 2 thick slices of sourdough
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • A handful of arugula
  • Flaky salt

Plus your protein and your flavor.

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. 2–3 min a sideHeat the olive oil in a skillet. Lay the bread in and press it down. Toast until golden and crisp on both sides.
  2. 3 minEggs: crack them into the same pan and cook until the whites set. Lamb: warm it through. Fish: nothing to cook.
  3. FinishSpread your flavor on the hot toast first, then the protein, then the arugula.
  4. Flaky salt over the top. Eat it right away.

Leftovers

Toast doesn't keep. Make it fresh.
