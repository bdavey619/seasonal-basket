Skillet hash

The week's leftovers, crisped in one pan with an egg cracked on top. A different dinner made from things you've already cooked.

20 minutes · Dinner for one

1 Pick a protein

  • Chicken sausage, leftover 2 links, sliced In with the pepper, about 5 minutes.
  • Pork tenderloin, leftover A few slices, diced In for the last 2 minutes.
  • Eggs only 2 extra eggs No meat — four eggs in the pan instead of two.

2 Pick a flavor

  • Smoky Ajvar + lemon A spoonful of the jar and its oil, lemon squeezed over everything. How to make the ajvar →
  • Sweet and salty Dates + feta Torn dates and crumbled feta, a pinch of chile so it isn't dessert.
  • Bright and hot Chile-garlic oil + lime Warm olive oil with a grated garlic clove and chile flakes for one minute. Pour it over; squeeze lime hard.

You'll need

  • 1 sweet potato or a few kabocha wedges, in ½-inch cubes — raw or leftover
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt

Plus your protein and your flavor.

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. Med-highHeat the oil in a heavy skillet until it shimmers.
  2. 5–10 minAdd the cubes in one layer and leave them alone until the bottoms brown. Leftover cubes need about 5 minutes; raw ones need 10, stirred once halfway.
  3. 5 minStir, then add the pepper and your protein. Cook until everything crisps at the edges.
  4. 2–3 minMake hollows in the hash, crack in the eggs, and cover the pan until the whites set.
  5. FinishYour flavor on top, and a pinch of salt.

Leftovers

Eat it from the pan. It's already made from leftovers.
