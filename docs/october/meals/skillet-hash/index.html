Skillet hash

The week's leftovers, crisped in one pan with an egg cracked on top. It's a different dinner made from things you've already cooked.

Keep

  • A heavy skillet
  • Eggs
  • Olive oil and salt

October adds

Start with
  • Sweet potato or kabocha, cubed — raw or leftover
  • Red pepper, diced
Choose from
  • Leftover sausage or pork tenderloin, sliced
  • Ajvar
  • Crumbled feta
  • Torn Medjool dates
  • Chile flakes
  • Arugula

Variations

Chicken sausage · smoky The default. Sliced sausage crisped with everything, ajvar at the table.
Pork tenderloin · sweet and salty Leftover pork in for the last minute, dates and feta on top.
Eggs only · bright and hot Two eggs instead of one, chile-garlic oil over the top.

Finish

Heat a heavy skillet with a good film of olive oil. Add the cubed squash or sweet potato in one layer and leave it alone until the bottom browns, about five minutes — the same hands-off rule as the fish. Stir, add the pepper and any leftover meat, and cook until everything is crisp at the edges. Make a hollow, crack in an egg, cover the pan for two or three minutes until the white sets.

Works well with

  • Toast
  • Arugula with lemon

From Field Notes

  • Dry fish, hot pan, hands off.

House Flavor
