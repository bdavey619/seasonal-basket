Seared rockfish, brown butter

A fish most people walk past, seared hard and finished in brown butter, over roasted kabocha, with a salad of arugula, pomegranate, dates, and feta that only works this month.

About 45 minutes · Dinner, and lunch tomorrow

You'll need

  • 1 lb rockfish fillets
  • ½ kabocha, in wedges
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 handfuls of arugula
  • Seeds of ½ pomegranate
  • 4 Medjool dates, pitted and torn
  • A little feta, crumbled
  • Olive oil and salt
  • Ajvar, for the table

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. 425°F · 30–35 minHeat the oven. Toss the kabocha with oil and salt and roast until the edges are dark and a knife slides through.
  2. 15 minSalt the fish and leave it on a plate, then pat it completely dry.
  3. Med-highHeat a heavy skillet over medium-high with a thin film of oil until it shimmers. Lay the fillets in, away from you.
  4. 3–4 minDon't touch them. They're ready to flip when the edges turn opaque and the fish lets go of the pan on its own.
  5. 1–2 minFlip, add the butter, and tilt the pan, spooning the foaming butter over the fish until it smells nutty and turns golden brown. Squeeze in half the lemon.
  6. Put the arugula, pomegranate, dates, and feta in a bowl. Dress it at the table with olive oil, the rest of the lemon, and salt.
  7. FinishFish over the kabocha, brown butter spooned on top, the salad beside it, ajvar on the table.

Leftovers

Leftover fish is tomorrow's tacos.

How it works

The sear is October's lesson: dry fish, a hot pan, and hands off until it lets go on its own — that's how a crust forms instead of sticking. Brown butter is the reward: butter cooked until its milk solids toast, which gives a mild fish everything it lacks.

Make it your own

  • Any firm white fillet — cod or halibut — works the same way.
  • Sweet potato instead of kabocha.
  • Skip the brown butter; finish with warm chile-garlic oil and lime instead.
