Sausages, peppers, and ajvar

Sausages roasted on one pan with squash and sweet peppers until everything browns in their fat — served the Balkan way, with a pile of ajvar on the side and bread to drag through it.

40 minutes, most of it in the oven · Dinner tonight and two lunches

You'll need

  • 1 lb chicken sausage, 4–5 links
  • ½ kabocha, in 1-inch wedges
  • 2 red bell peppers, in thick strips
  • Ajvar, a few big spoonfuls
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt
  • Sourdough, to serve

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. 425°FHeat the oven. If the kabocha is too hard to cut, put it in whole for 10 minutes first.
  2. Toss the squash and peppers with the olive oil and a big pinch of salt and spread them on a sheet pan with room between the pieces. Add the sausages.
  3. 30 minRoast, turning the sausages once, until they're browned and the squash edges are dark.
  4. Last 5 minToast the sourdough in a skillet with a little oil while the pan finishes.
  5. FinishEverything on the plate, a big spoonful of ajvar beside it, lemon squeezed over the vegetables.

Leftovers

Pack two lunches before you sit down: sliced sausage, squash, and peppers over rice, ajvar on the side.

How it works

Sausage brings its own fat, and on a hot sheet pan that fat runs out and roasts the vegetables around it — the pan seasons itself. Ajvar is what's served with grilled sausages across the Balkans: smoky, sweet, a little sharp, exactly what cuts the richness.

Make it your own

  • Any sausage works — pork, lamb, or chicken.
  • Sweet potato instead of kabocha, same timing.
  • No ajvar made yet? Lemon, olive oil, and a pinch of chile flakes over the top.
