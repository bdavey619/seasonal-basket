Rice bowl

October's rice bowl is built on two things you made once: a tray of roasted kabocha and a jar of muhammara.

Keep

  • Sticky white rice
  • Protein of choice (chicken, beef, beans, or nothing)

October adds

Start with
  • Roasted kabocha wedges
  • A big spoonful of muhammara
Choose from
  • Arugula, a handful
  • Pomegranate seeds
  • Toasted walnuts
  • Salted Greek yogurt

Variations

Kabocha + muhammara + arugula The version you'll make most often. Three minutes if the tray is in the fridge.
Kabocha + yogurt + pomegranate Cold and hot, sweet and sour. No protein needed.
Chicken thighs + muhammara + arugula + walnuts The filling one, for after a long day.

Finish

Pomegranate last, over everything, so it stays cold and bright against the warm rice. If the bowl tastes flat, it wants salt or another spoonful of the jar.

Works well with

  • Chicken thighs
  • Ground beef or turkey
  • Chickpeas or white beans
  • A fried egg

From Field Notes

  • Let the oven cut the kabocha.

House Flavor

  • Muhammara
