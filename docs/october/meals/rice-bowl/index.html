October · weekday

Rice bowl

October's rice bowl is built on two things you made once: a tray of roasted sweet potato and a jar of salsa macha.

Keep

  • Sticky white rice
  • Protein of choice (beef, chicken, beans, or nothing)

October adds

Start with
  • Roasted sweet potato
  • A spoonful of salsa macha, with its oil
Choose from
  • Charred poblano strips
  • Roasted delicata
  • Pomegranate seeds
  • Lime

Variations

Sweet potato + salsa macha + pomegranate The version you'll make most often. Three minutes if the tray is in the fridge.
Ground beef + charred poblano + sweet potato The last of the chiles, doing their job.
Leftover braised chicken + white beans + lemon The weekend's pot, Monday's bowl.

Finish

Something sharp, last — pomegranate or lime. October food is heavier than September's, and the squeeze matters more, not less.

Works well with

  • Ground beef or turkey
  • Leftover braised chicken
  • Black or white beans
  • A fried egg

From Field Notes

  • An ancho is a poblano that waited.
  • Seed a pomegranate underwater.

House Flavor

  • Salsa macha
