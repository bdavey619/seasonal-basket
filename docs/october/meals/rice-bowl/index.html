Rice bowl

October's rice bowl is built from a tray of roasted sweet potato and the last charred poblanos. Something sharp goes on top.

Keep

  • Sticky white rice
  • Ground beef or turkey, or leftover braised chicken

October adds

Start with
  • Roasted sweet potato
  • Charred poblano strips
Choose from
  • Pomegranate seeds
  • Roasted delicata
  • Lime or lemon
  • Greek yogurt, salted

Variations

Ground beef + poblano + sweet potato + pomegranate The one you'll make most. Sweet, smoke, something sharp.
Braised chicken + white beans + lemon Sunday's pot, Monday's bowl.

Finish

Something sharp, last, every time — pomegranate, lime, or lemon. October food is heavier than September's, and the squeeze matters more, not less.

Works well with

  • Ground beef or turkey
  • Leftover braised chicken
  • A fried egg
  • White or black beans
