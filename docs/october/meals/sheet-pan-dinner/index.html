Sheet-pan dinner

One vegetable, one protein, one pan, a hot oven. The flavor you finish with decides which dinner it is.

About 40 minutes, most of it in the oven · Dinner tonight, two lunches

1 Pick a protein

  • Chicken sausage 1 lb, 4–5 links On the pan with the vegetables from the start. About 30 minutes, turned once.
  • Pork tenderloin 1, about 1¼ lbs Joins the pan after 10 minutes. Out when the center is just pink (145°F), 20–25 minutes. Rest 5 minutes before slicing.

2 Pick a flavor

  • Smoky Ajvar + lemon A spoonful of the jar and its oil, lemon squeezed over everything.
  • Sweet and salty Dates + feta Torn dates and crumbled feta, a pinch of chile so it isn't dessert.
  • Bright and hot Chile-garlic oil + lime Warm olive oil with a grated garlic clove and chile flakes for one minute. Pour it over; squeeze lime hard.

You'll need

  • ½ kabocha, in 1-inch wedges — or 2 sweet potatoes, cubed
  • 2 red bell peppers, in thick strips
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt

Plus your protein and your flavor.

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. 425°FHeat the oven. If the kabocha is too hard to cut, put it in whole for 10 minutes first.
  2. Toss the squash and peppers with the olive oil and a big pinch of salt. Spread them on a sheet pan with room between the pieces — use two pans if they touch.
  3. StartSausage: add the links to the pan now. Pork: rub the tenderloin with oil and salt and set it aside.
  4. 10 minRoast. Pork: add the tenderloin to the pan when the time's up.
  5. 20 min moreRoast until the squash edges are dark and the sausage is browned — turn it once. Pork is ready when the center is just pink; rest it 5 minutes, then slice thick.
  6. FinishPlate it and finish with your flavor.

Leftovers

Pack two lunches before you sit down: squash, peppers, and sliced protein over rice. Add the flavor when you eat it.

Good alongside

  • Rice
  • Black beans
  • Arugula, a handful on top

From Field Notes

  • Let the oven cut the kabocha.

House Flavor
