Sheet-pan dinner

One vegetable, one protein, one pan, a hot oven. Sausage or pork tenderloin both work; the flavor you finish with decides which dinner it is.

Keep

  • A sheet pan
  • Olive oil and salt

October adds

Start with
  • Chicken sausage, or a whole pork tenderloin
  • Kabocha wedges, or sweet potato in cubes
  • Red pepper strips
Choose from
  • Ajvar
  • Salted Greek yogurt
  • Pomegranate seeds
  • Lemon or lime
  • Chile flakes and garlic

Variations

Chicken sausage · smoky The default. Ajvar and lemon over everything.
Pork tenderloin · sweet and salty Sliced thick over the squash, torn dates and feta on top.
Chicken sausage · bright and hot Chile-garlic oil poured over at the table, lime squeezed hard.

Finish

Heat the oven to 425°F. Squash and peppers go on the pan first, oiled and salted, with room between the pieces. Sausage goes in with them for about 30 minutes, turned once. A pork tenderloin, salted and oiled, joins the pan for the last 20 to 25 minutes — take it out when the center is still blushing pink (145°F if you have a thermometer) and let it rest five minutes before slicing. Finish with one of October's flavors.

Works well with

  • Rice
  • Black beans
  • Arugula, a handful on top

From Field Notes

  • Let the oven cut the kabocha.

House Flavor

  • Ajvar
