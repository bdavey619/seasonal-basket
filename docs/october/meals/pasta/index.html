Pasta

Arugula wilts into hot pasta in about five seconds. With lemon and a lot of parmesan, it's the fastest dinner in this edition.

Keep

  • Dried pasta — something short, or spaghetti
  • Olive oil or butter
  • Parmesan
  • Salt and black pepper

October adds

Start with
  • 2 big handfuls of arugula
  • 1 lemon, zest and juice
Choose from
  • A spoonful of ajvar
  • Roasted kabocha, cubed
  • Chile flakes
  • Toasted breadcrumbs, for crunch

Variations

Arugula + lemon + parmesan The base version. Toss the arugula in off the heat so it wilts without cooking.
Ajvar + arugula Loosen a few spoonfuls of the jar with pasta water. A red sauce that isn't tomato.
Kabocha + brown butter + breadcrumbs Richer. The one that eats like a weekend.

Finish

Save a mug of pasta water before draining — parmesan and oil need it to become a sauce. Arugula goes in after the heat is off, then lemon, then more parmesan than seems right.

Works well with

  • Nothing at all
  • White beans, warmed in the oil
  • Chicken thighs, sliced
  • A fried egg

House Flavor

  • Ajvar
