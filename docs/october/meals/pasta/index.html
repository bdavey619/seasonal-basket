October · weekday

Pasta

Roast the delicata on Sunday and this is a ten-minute dinner any night after. Butter, sage, squash, lemon.

Keep

  • Dried pasta — something short
  • Butter
  • Parmesan
  • Salt

October adds

Start with
  • Roasted delicata half-moons
  • A few sage leaves
Choose from
  • Lemon zest
  • White beans, warmed in the butter
  • Charred poblano strips
  • Black pepper, a lot of it

Variations

Delicata + brown butter + sage Melt the butter with the sage until it smells like toast, then fold in the squash.
Delicata + white beans + lemon Heartier. The beans turn creamy in the pasta water.

Finish

Save a mug of pasta water. Butter and parmesan need it to become a sauce rather than a coating. Lemon zest goes on off the heat.

Works well with

  • Nothing at all
  • Leftover chicken, pulled
  • A fried egg on top
