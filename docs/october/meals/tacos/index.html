Tacos

Roasted sweet potato, black beans, pomegranate, cotija. Sweet, sharp, and salty in one bite.

Keep

  • Corn tortillas
  • Black beans, or protein of choice
  • Cotija or crumbled feta
  • Lime

October adds

Start with
  • Roasted sweet potato, cubed
  • Pomegranate seeds
Choose from
  • A smear of ajvar
  • Pepper strips, softened in oil
  • Guava, diced small, with lime
  • Salted yogurt, in place of crema

Variations

Sweet potato + black beans + pomegranate + cotija The October taco. The pomegranate is the salsa.
Ajvar + sweet potato + arugula Smear the jar on the warm tortilla first.
Rockfish + arugula + pomegranate + lime Weekend leftovers, or fillets seared three minutes a side.

Finish

Warm the tortillas over a flame or in a dry pan until they blister in spots, and stack them under a towel. Pomegranate goes on last so it stays cold against the warm sweet potato.

Works well with

  • Rockfish, pan-seared and flaked
  • Black or pinto beans
  • Ground beef or turkey

From Field Notes

  • Let the oven cut the kabocha.
  • Cook it on the bone.
