Fish tacos

San Diego's taco, made with this month's fish. Seared, not fried, and finished in the time it takes to warm the tortillas.

Keep

  • Corn tortillas
  • Lime

October adds

Start with
  • Seared rockfish, flaked
  • Arugula, in place of lettuce
Choose from
  • Salted Greek yogurt
  • Pomegranate seeds
  • Ajvar
  • Crumbled feta
  • Chile flakes and garlic

Variations

Rockfish · cool and sharp The default. Salted yogurt and pomegranate. The pomegranate is the salsa.
Rockfish · bright and hot Chile-garlic oil over the fish, lime squeezed hard, arugula.
Rockfish · smoky Smear ajvar on the warm tortilla first, fish on top, feta last.

Finish

Warm the tortillas in a dry pan until they blister in spots, and stack them under a towel. Two per taco. Sear the fish the same way as the weekend — dry, hot pan, hands off — then flake it straight into the tortillas.

Works well with

  • Black beans
  • Rice
  • Lime, a lot of it

From Field Notes

  • Dry fish, hot pan, hands off.

House Flavor

  • Ajvar
