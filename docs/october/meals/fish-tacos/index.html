Fish tacos

San Diego's taco, made with this month's fish. Seared, not fried, and finished in the time it takes to warm the tortillas.

15 minutes · Dinner for one, about 6 tacos

1 Pick a protein

  • Rockfish 1 lb fillets Seared 3–4 minutes without moving, flipped, 1–2 minutes more. Flaked in the pan.

2 Pick a flavor

  • Cool and sharp Salted yogurt + pomegranate Yogurt on the plate first, the hot food on top, seeds over it all.
  • Bright and hot Chile-garlic oil + lime Warm olive oil with a grated garlic clove and chile flakes for one minute. Pour it over; squeeze lime hard.
  • Smoky Ajvar + lemon A spoonful of the jar and its oil, lemon squeezed over everything. How to make the ajvar →

You'll need

  • 12 corn tortillas — two per taco
  • 2 handfuls of arugula
  • 1 lime
  • Olive oil and salt

Plus your protein and your flavor.

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. 10 minSalt the fish and leave it on a plate. Pat it completely dry right before it goes in the pan.
  2. TortillasWarm the tortillas in a dry pan until they blister in spots. Stack them under a towel.
  3. Med-highHeat a thin film of oil in a heavy skillet until it shimmers. Lay the fish in, away from you.
  4. 3–4 minDon't touch it. It's ready to flip when it lets go of the pan on its own. Flip, then 1–2 minutes more until it's opaque all the way through.
  5. Flake the fish in the pan with a fork.
  6. FinishTwo tortillas, fish, arugula, then your flavor. Lime last.

Leftovers

Leftover fish is good cold the next day, on toast with lemon and olive oil.
