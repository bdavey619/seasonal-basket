Baked sweet potato

Bake it until the skin puffs and the sugars run, split it, and fill it. The potato is the whole dinner.

About an hour, almost all of it in the oven · Dinner tonight — bake extra for the hash

1 Pick a protein

  • Black beans 1 can, drained Warmed in a small pan, 5 minutes.
  • Lamb or sausage, leftover A big spoonful Warmed in a pan, 2–3 minutes.
  • No protein — Dates and feta carry it. Use the sweet and salty flavor.

2 Pick a flavor

  • Smoky Ajvar + lemon A spoonful of the jar and its oil, lemon squeezed over everything. How to make the ajvar →
  • Sweet and salty Dates + feta Torn dates and crumbled feta, a pinch of chile so it isn't dessert.
  • Cool and sharp Salted yogurt + pomegranate Yogurt on the plate first, the hot food on top, seeds over it all.

You'll need

  • 2 sweet potatoes for dinner — bake 4, the extra two are the hash
  • Olive oil and salt
  • Salted Greek yogurt, a big spoonful per potato

Plus your protein and your flavor.

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. 400°FHeat the oven. Scrub the potatoes, prick each a few times with a fork, rub with oil and salt, and set them on a sheet pan.
  2. About 1 hrBake until the skin puffs away from the flesh and syrup beads out and caramelizes on the pan. Soft isn't done — keep going.
  3. Last 5 minWarm the beans or the leftover protein.
  4. Split each potato lengthwise, push the ends toward the middle so it opens, and salt the inside.
  5. FinishYogurt, then the beans or protein, then your flavor.

Leftovers

Baked sweet potatoes keep four days. Cube them cold for the skillet hash.
