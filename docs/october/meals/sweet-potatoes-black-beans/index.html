Sweet potatoes and black beans

Sweet potatoes baked until their sugars run and caramelize on the pan, split open and filled with warm black beans, smoky ajvar, cold lime yogurt, and feta.

About an hour, almost all of it in the oven · Dinner tonight, two more potatoes for later

You'll need

  • 4 sweet potatoes — two for tonight, two for later
  • 1 can black beans, drained
  • Ajvar, a few spoonfuls
  • ½ cup Greek yogurt
  • 1 lime
  • Feta, crumbled
  • Olive oil and salt

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. 400°FHeat the oven. Scrub the potatoes, prick each a few times, rub with oil and salt, and set them on a sheet pan.
  2. About 1 hrBake until the skin puffs away from the flesh and syrup beads out and caramelizes on the pan. Soft isn't done — keep going.
  3. Stir the lime zest, half its juice, and a pinch of salt into the yogurt.
  4. Last 5 minWarm the beans in a small pan with a spoonful of ajvar and a pinch of salt.
  5. Split each potato lengthwise, push the ends toward the middle so it opens, and salt the inside.
  6. FinishBeans, then a spoonful of lime yogurt, then feta and more ajvar. The rest of the lime over the top.

Leftovers

The extra potatoes keep four days. Reheat them whole, or cube them cold and crisp them in a skillet with an egg on top.

How it works

A sweet potato baked long enough is rich and sweet on its own, so it wants smoke, salt, and acid against it: ajvar, feta, lime yogurt. The real technique is patience — soft isn't done; keep going until the syrup caramelizes on the pan.

Make it your own

  • Leftover lamb or sausage instead of the beans.
  • Torn dates and feta for a sweet-and-salty version.
  • Kabocha wedges work the same way, in half the time.
