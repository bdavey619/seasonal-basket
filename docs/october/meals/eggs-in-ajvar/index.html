Eggs in ajvar

The month's jar, loosened with a little water, becomes a skillet of smoky pepper sauce. Four eggs set in it, feta goes over the top, and sourdough does the rest.

20 minutes · Dinner for one, generously

You'll need

  • Ajvar, about ¾ cup
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • 2 oz feta, crumbled
  • A handful of arugula
  • 2 thick slices of sourdough
  • Olive oil, chile flakes, and salt

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. 1 minWarm 1 tbsp olive oil in a small skillet over medium. Add the garlic and a pinch of chile flakes and stir until it smells good — don't let it brown.
  2. 3 minAdd the ajvar and ½ cup water, stir, and simmer until it's loose and bubbling, like a thick tomato sauce.
  3. Make four wells in the sauce with the back of a spoon, crack an egg into each, and salt the eggs.
  4. Covered · 5–7 minCover the pan and cook over medium-low until the whites are set and the yolks still wobble. Check at 5 minutes; they go from runny to hard quickly.
  5. Meanwhile, toast the sourdough in a dry pan or under the broiler, and drizzle it with olive oil.
  6. FinishFeta and arugula over the pan, a little olive oil, and the toast torn alongside for dragging through the sauce.

Leftovers

Eggs don't reheat well, but extra sauce keeps for days. Make more and poach fresh eggs in it tomorrow.

How it works

This is shakshuka's method with a different sauce: any thick, flavorful sauce can poach eggs if you loosen it to the texture of a tomato sauce and cover the pan, so the tops cook in steam while the bottoms stay soft. The salty feta and the bread are there because the sauce is sweet and smoky and wants something to cut it and something to carry it.

Make it your own

  • Crumble in leftover lamb or sausage before the eggs go in.
  • A spoonful of salted yogurt instead of feta.
  • No bread? Spoon it over rice.

Checked against

  • Shakshuka — America's Test Kitchen — eggs set in wells in a pepper sauce, covered on the stove until the whites set
  • 20-Minute Shakshuka — Food52 — the fast version: red peppers, four eggs, covered until set, feta on top
  • Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart — USDA — eggs are safe once the whites are firm; the yolks are your call
