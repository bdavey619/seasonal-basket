Ajvar toast with eggs

Sourdough fried crisp in olive oil, spread thick with smoky ajvar, topped with two runny eggs and a pile of lemony arugula. The jar does the work.

10 minutes · Dinner for one

You'll need

  • 2 thick slices of sourdough
  • Ajvar, 2–3 big spoonfuls
  • 2 eggs
  • A handful of arugula
  • ½ lemon
  • Olive oil and flaky salt

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. 2–3 min a sideHeat 2 tbsp olive oil in a skillet. Lay the bread in, press it down, and toast until golden and crisp on both sides.
  2. 3 minCrack the eggs into the same pan and cook until the whites set and the yolks are still runny.
  3. Spread the ajvar thick on the hot toast.
  4. Toss the arugula with lemon, olive oil, and a pinch of salt.
  5. FinishEgg on each toast, arugula over it, flaky salt.

Leftovers

Toast doesn't keep. Make it fresh.

How it works

A great jar turns two eggs into dinner. Ajvar is smoky and sweet, the runny yolk turns it into a sauce, and lemony arugula keeps it from feeling heavy. Any thick, flavorful spread works the same way — this is a template for every month's jar.

Make it your own

  • Leftover lamb or flaked rockfish on top instead of the eggs.
  • A spoonful of salted yogurt under the ajvar for something cool.
  • Crumble feta over the top.
