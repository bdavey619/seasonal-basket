Crispy lamb over yogurt

Lamb browned until it crackles, spooned hot over cold salted yogurt, with sweet roasted squash, dates, and pomegranate. Rich, sour, sweet, and crisp in every forkful.

35 minutes, or 15 if the squash is already roasted · Dinner tonight and a second plate

You'll need

  • 1 lb ground lamb
  • ½ kabocha, in 1-inch wedges
  • 1 cup Greek yogurt
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • Chile flakes
  • Seeds of ½ pomegranate
  • 4 Medjool dates, pitted and torn
  • A big handful of arugula
  • ½ lemon
  • Olive oil and salt
  • Rice or toasted sourdough, to serve

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. 425°F · 30 minHeat the oven. Toss the kabocha with oil and salt and roast until the edges are dark and a knife slides through.
  2. Stir a big pinch of salt and a squeeze of lemon into the yogurt. Spread half of it on a plate.
  3. 4–5 minPut the lamb in a dry skillet over medium-high, press it flat, and don't touch it until it crusts underneath.
  4. 4–5 minBreak it up and keep cooking until it's browned all over and crackling at the edges.
  5. 1 minAdd the garlic, a pinch of chile flakes, and salt for the last minute, and stir the dates in to warm them.
  6. FinishSquash on the yogurt, lamb over it, pomegranate and arugula on top, lemon squeezed over everything.

Leftovers

Half the lamb keeps four days. Tomorrow it's toast for dinner, or the filling for a baked sweet potato.

How it works

Rich meat wants something sour and something sweet against it. Here the yogurt and pomegranate cut the fat, and the dates and squash round it out. Browning the lamb hard — flat in the pan, left alone — turns its fat into crisp edges, which is most of the flavor.

Make it your own

  • No lamb? Ground beef works the same way, or leftover pork tenderloin, sliced and warmed.
  • No dates? Double the pomegranate.
  • No roasted squash? Serve it over rice and let the yogurt carry it.
