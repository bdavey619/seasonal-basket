Crispy rice with sausage

Leftover rice pressed into a hot pan and left alone until the bottom turns into a golden crust, with browned sausage, two fried eggs, and a spoonful of chile-garlic oil. The best use of yesterday's rice.

20 minutes · Dinner for one

You'll need

  • 2 cups cooked rice, cold — day-old is best
  • 2 chicken sausages, sliced into coins
  • 2 eggs
  • A handful of arugula
  • 1 lime
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • Olive oil, chile flakes, and salt

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. 1 minMake the chile-garlic oil: warm 3 tbsp olive oil with the garlic and a big pinch of chile flakes in a small pan until it sizzles, then take it off the heat.
  2. 5 minHeat 1 tbsp oil in a large nonstick or well-seasoned skillet over medium-high. Brown the sausage coins on both sides and move them to a plate. Raw sausage should reach 165°F.
  3. Break up the cold rice with your fingers and toss it with 1 tbsp oil and a pinch of salt so every grain is coated.
  4. 6–8 minSpread the rice in the same pan and press it flat. Don't stir. It's ready when the edges turn golden and the rice crackles and lifts from the pan.
  5. 3 minPush the rice to one side, or use a second pan, and fry the eggs in a little oil until the whites are crisp at the edges and the yolks are runny.
  6. FinishBreak the crust into the bowl, sausage over it, eggs on top, arugula, the chile-garlic oil spooned over everything, and lime squeezed over the top.

Leftovers

Eat it right away. It's the leftover dish.

How it works

Cold rice crisps because it has dried out: oil coats each grain, and a hot pan you leave alone turns the bottom layer into a crust instead of mush. It's the same hands-off lesson as the fish — the crust forms when you stop moving it. The runny yolk and the lime are the sauce.

Make it your own

  • Leftover pork tenderloin or lamb instead of sausage.
  • Ajvar instead of chile-garlic oil.
  • Add cubes of leftover roasted kabocha with the rice.
