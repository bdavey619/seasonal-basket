Orzo salad

Make it on Sunday and it carries the week: orzo dressed while it's hot, dark-roasted squash, salty feta. Arugula and pomegranate go on each bowl as you eat it, so it tastes new on day four.

45 minutes, most of it roasting and cooling · Five snack bowls, or three lunches

About 750–850 kcal · 15–20 g protein a serving (estimate)

You'll need

  • 8 oz orzo (half a box)
  • ½ kabocha, in ¾-inch cubes
  • 3 oz feta, crumbled
  • ⅓ cup olive oil, plus more for the squash
  • 3 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • ½ lemon
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • Chile flakes and salt
  • To serve: arugula and pomegranate seeds

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. 425°F · 25–30 minHeat the oven. Toss the squash with oil and salt, spread it on a sheet pan in one layer, and roast, flipping once, until the edges are dark and it's tender. Let it cool.
  2. Whisk the olive oil, vinegar, lemon juice, garlic, a pinch of chile flakes, and ½ tsp salt in a large bowl. Spoon 2 tbsp of it into a small jar and keep it for serving.
  3. About 10 minBoil the orzo in well-salted water — it should taste like the sea — for 2 minutes longer than the package says. Cold pasta firms up, so it needs the extra time.
  4. Drain it without rinsing and toss it hot with the dressing in the bowl. Hot pasta drinks the dressing; cold pasta only gets coated.
  5. 15 minSpread the dressed orzo on a sheet pan to cool so it doesn't clump.
  6. Fold in the cooled squash and the feta, pack it into a covered container, and refrigerate it within 2 hours. Keep the arugula and pomegranate out.
  7. To eatScoop a bowl, add a handful of arugula and some pomegranate, a spoonful of the saved dressing, and taste: cold dulls flavor, so it usually wants a squeeze of lemon and a pinch of salt.

Leftovers

It is the leftovers. Covered in the fridge, it keeps 3 to 4 days.

Make it a meal

For a lunch, halve two soft-boiled eggs over the bowl and crumble on a little more feta. The yolks dress the orzo.

How it works

Three things make a pasta salad last: dress the pasta while it's hot so the flavor goes in, not just on; cook it a little past al dente because the cold firms it; and keep anything delicate out of the container until you eat it. The formula works every month — orzo, the month's vegetable, one cheese, a sharp dressing, something fresh at the end.

Make it your own

  • Leftover roasted sweet potato in place of the kabocha.
  • Whisk a spoonful of ajvar into the dressing for smoke.
  • Torn dates for sweetness against the feta.
