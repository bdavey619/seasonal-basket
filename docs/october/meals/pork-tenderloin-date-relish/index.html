Pork tenderloin with date-feta relish

A whole tenderloin roasted beside sweet potatoes, sliced while it's still blushing pink, and covered in a salty, sweet relish of dates, feta, lemon, and chile.

40 minutes · Dinner tonight and two lunches

You'll need

  • 1 pork tenderloin, about 1¼ lbs
  • 2 sweet potatoes, in 1-inch cubes
  • 1 red bell pepper, in strips
  • 6 Medjool dates, pitted and chopped
  • 2 oz feta, crumbled
  • 1 lemon
  • Chile flakes
  • Olive oil and salt
  • Arugula, to serve

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. 425°FHeat the oven. Toss the sweet potatoes and pepper with oil and salt and spread them on a sheet pan.
  2. 10 minRoast the vegetables on their own first.
  3. 20–25 minRub the pork with oil and salt and lay it in the middle of the pan. Roast until the center is just pink.
  4. Meanwhile, make the relish: the dates, the feta, the zest and juice of the lemon, a pinch of chile flakes, and 3 tbsp olive oil, stirred together.
  5. Rest 5 minTake the pork out at 145°F, or when it's just pink in the middle, and let it rest before slicing.
  6. FinishSlice it thick over the sweet potatoes and arugula, and spoon the relish over everything.

Leftovers

Sliced pork and sweet potatoes over rice, with the leftover relish, is two lunches.

How it works

Lean meat has almost no fat to keep it juicy or carry flavor, so the richness goes on at the end instead: a relish of olive oil, salty feta, and sweet dates does what a fattier cut would do on its own. The other half of the lesson is pulling it early — pork is at its best slightly pink.

Make it your own

  • Thick pork chops, seared hard, take the same relish.
  • No feta? Salt the relish a little more.
  • Extra relish is good on roasted squash or spread on toast.
