Warm plate

Something warm, something cold, something sharp, on one plate. Lamb or leftover pork makes it dinner; the flavor makes it different each time.

20 minutes if the squash is already roasted, 45 if not · Dinner, plus a second plate or two lunches

1 Pick a protein

  • Ground lamb 1 lb Browned hard in a dry pan, 8–10 minutes. Half tonight, half keeps four days.
  • Pork tenderloin, leftover A few slices Warmed in the pan, 2 minutes.

2 Pick a flavor

  • Sweet and salty Dates + feta Torn dates and crumbled feta, a pinch of chile so it isn't dessert.
  • Cool and sharp Salted yogurt + pomegranate Yogurt on the plate first, the hot food on top, seeds over it all.
  • Smoky Ajvar + lemon A spoonful of the jar and its oil, lemon squeezed over everything. How to make the ajvar →

You'll need

  • Rice, about 1 cup cooked per plate — leftover is fine
  • Roasted kabocha, about ¼ squash per plate
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • A pinch of chile flakes
  • Arugula, a handful per plate
  • Salt

Plus your protein and your flavor.

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. If neededNo roasted squash yet? Wedges, olive oil, salt, 425°F, 30–35 minutes. Start the rice.
  2. 4 minLamb: put it in a dry skillet over medium-high, press it flat, and leave it alone until it crusts underneath. Pork: warm the slices 2 minutes and skip to step 4.
  3. 4–5 minBreak the lamb up and cook until browned all over.
  4. 1 minAdd the garlic and chile flakes for the last minute. Salt it.
  5. Build the plate in layers: rice, warm squash and protein, arugula on the side.
  6. FinishAdd your flavor on top.

Leftovers

Half the lamb keeps four days in the fridge. It's a second plate, toast for dinner, or the filling for a baked sweet potato.
