Warm plate

Something warm, something cold, something sharp, on one plate. Ground lamb makes it dinner; the rest makes it October.

Keep

  • Rice, warm or leftover
  • Greek yogurt, salted
  • Olive oil, lemon, salt

October adds

Start with
  • Ground lamb, browned with garlic and chile flakes
  • Roasted kabocha
Choose from
  • Pomegranate seeds
  • Torn Medjool dates
  • Arugula, a handful
  • Crumbled feta

Variations

Lamb + kabocha + yogurt + pomegranate The default. Yogurt under everything, pomegranate over it.
Lamb + dates + arugula + lemon Sweeter, with something green. The dates warm through in the pan.
Kabocha + arugula + feta + pomegranate No lamb left. Still a whole plate.

Finish

Brown the lamb hard in a dry pan — let it sit until it crusts before you break it up — then add a grated garlic clove and a pinch of chile flakes for the last minute. Build the plate in layers: salted yogurt, rice, warm squash and lamb, then everything sharp on top.

Works well with

  • Rice
  • Black beans
  • A fried egg

From Field Notes

  • Dry fish, hot pan, hands off.
  • A new date isn't dried fruit.

House Flavor
