October

Keep the meal. Change the season.

October is San Diego's second summer. Some years the hottest week lands now, when the wind blows in from the desert. But the harvest has moved inland — new dates, new sweet potatoes, heavy pomegranates, the last sweet peppers — and as the water cools, the local rockfish comes in. This is the month to buy a fish whole.

One thing to notice

A fresh fish looks back at you.

At the counter, look at the eyes before anything else. Clear, bright, and slightly bulging means it came in recently; cloudy and sunken means it didn't. Then ask to see under the gill flap: bright red is fresh, brown is not. It takes five seconds and tells you more than any label.
