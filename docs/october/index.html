October

Keep the meal. Change the season.

October is San Diego's second summer. Some years the hottest week lands now, when the wind blows in from the desert. But the harvest has moved inland — new dates, new sweet potatoes, heavy pomegranates, the last sweet peppers — and as the water cools, the local rockfish comes in. Everything below comes from one store, and it all keeps you cooking through the week.

October's four flavors
  • Smoky Ajvar + lemon A spoonful of the jar and its oil, lemon squeezed over everything.
  • Cool and sharp Salted yogurt + pomegranate Yogurt on the plate first, the hot food on top, seeds over it all.

One thing to notice

A fresh fillet is glossy.

At the fish counter, look before you ask. Fresh fish is glossy and slightly translucent, with no gaps opening between the layers of flesh. Dull, dry, or separating means it's been sitting. Ask to smell it; it should smell like the ocean and nothing else. It takes five seconds and tells you more than any label.
