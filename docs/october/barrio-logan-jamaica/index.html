The drink · October

Barrio Logan Jamaica

One drink. It's still hot, and the pomegranates just came in.

Keep

  • Sugar
  • Cold water
  • Ice

October adds

  • 8 hibiscus tea bags
  • 1 pomegranate
  • 1 lime

Method

Put the tea bags in four cups of cold water and leave them in the fridge overnight — cold-steeped, hibiscus comes out bright rather than bitter. Take out the bags. Press the seeds from one pomegranate through a strainer and stir the juice in, with sugar to taste; start with a quarter cup and stop when it's tart-sweet, not sweet. Pour over ice with a squeeze of lime and a spoonful of whole seeds. The pitcher keeps four days.

Try another way

Half jamaica, half cold water Lighter, for the hottest afternoons.
Lemon instead of lime Softer and a little rounder.
Freeze some into ice cubes So the last glass isn't watered down.
Less sugar, more seeds If you want it closer to tart.
