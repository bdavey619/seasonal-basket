The drink · October

Barrio Logan Jamaica

One drink. It's still hot, and the pomegranates just came in.

Keep

  • Dried hibiscus flowers (flor de jamaica)
  • Sugar
  • Cold water
  • Ice

October adds

  • 1 pomegranate, seeded
  • 1 lime

Method

Cover a cup of dried hibiscus with four cups of cold water and leave it in the fridge overnight — cold-steeped, it comes out bright rather than bitter. Strain. Press the seeds from one pomegranate through a strainer and stir the juice in, with sugar to taste; start with a quarter cup and stop when it's tart-sweet, not sweet. Pour over ice with a squeeze of lime and a spoonful of whole seeds. The pitcher keeps four days.

Try another way

Top with sparkling water If you want it lighter and longer.
A thin slice of guava in the glass Late in the month, when the guavas are soft.
A splash of mezcal or tequila If it's Friday.
Tajín on the rim If you want it to taste like the corner stand.
