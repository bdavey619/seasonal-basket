The drink · October

South Park Shrub

One drink. Make the base once and it lasts the week.

Keep

  • Apple cider vinegar
  • Sugar
  • Sparkling water
  • Ice

October adds

  • 1 pomegranate, seeded
  • A thin slice of apple, optional

Method

Press the seeds from one pomegranate through a strainer to get about half a cup of juice; save a spoonful of whole seeds. Stir the juice with two tablespoons of cider vinegar and two tablespoons of sugar until the sugar dissolves. That's the shrub — it keeps a week in the fridge. For a glass: two tablespoons over ice, topped with sparkling water, a few seeds on top. It should taste sharp first and sweet second.

Try another way

Add a shot of mezcal If it's Friday.
Top with cold apple cider instead of soda When the evening actually turns cool.
Salt the rim with Tajín If you miss September.
A splash of shrub in plain seltzer In the afternoon, instead of anything sweeter.
