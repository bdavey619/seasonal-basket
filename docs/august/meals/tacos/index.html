August · weekday

Tacos

Peppers, jalapeño, cherry tomato, cotija, lime. This is the taco that August makes possible.

Keep

  • Corn or flour tortillas
  • Protein of choice
  • Cotija or crumbled feta
  • Lime

August adds

Start with
  • Roasted or raw bell pepper strips
  • Jalapeño, sliced thin
Choose from
  • Cherry tomatoes, halved
  • Fresh basil

Variations

Peppers + jalapeño + cherry tomato + cotija The August version. Brighter than it looks.
Peppers + jalapeño + charred pepper sauce Use the house flavor as the salsa.
Zucchini + pepper + lime + cotija If you want a vegetable-forward taco.

Finish

Warm the tortillas directly over a gas flame or in a dry pan. Stack them and wrap in a kitchen towel while you assemble everything else. A taco with a warm tortilla is a different thing.

Works well with

  • Ground beef or turkey
  • Chicken thighs, pulled
  • Black beans
  • Fish

From Field Notes

House Flavor

  • Charred Pepper Sauce
