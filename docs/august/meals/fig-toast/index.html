Fig toast

Thick sourdough, torn burrata, ripe figs, honey, flaky salt. The best five-minute dinner of the summer.

Keep

  • Sourdough, thick slices
  • Olive oil, honey, flaky salt

August adds

Start with
  • Ripe figs, halved
Choose from
  • Burrata
  • Prosciutto
  • Leftover pork chop, sliced thin
  • Nectarine
  • Basil

Variations

Burrata · sweet The default. Burrata, figs, honey, salt.
Prosciutto · sweet Prosciutto torn over the burrata and figs. The old August weekend plate, on toast.
Burrata · hot A few rings of jalapeño over the figs. Strange and very good.

Finish

Toast the bread in a skillet with olive oil until crisp. Tear the burrata over it while it's hot so it starts to soften, then the figs, a thread of honey, and flaky salt.

Works well with

  • A peeled nectarine
  • Black pepper
