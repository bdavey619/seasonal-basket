Fig toast

Thick sourdough, torn burrata, ripe figs, honey, flaky salt. The best five-minute dinner of the summer.

10 minutes · Dinner for one

1 Pick a protein

  • Burrata ½ ball Torn over the hot toast so it starts to soften.
  • Prosciutto 2–3 slices Draped over the burrata and figs. Nothing to cook.
  • Pork chop, leftover A few thin slices Under the figs, cold or barely warmed.

2 Pick a flavor

  • Sweet Nectarine or fig + flaky salt Sliced ripe fruit, olive oil, flaky salt. Next to anything charred.
  • Hot Jalapeño + lime + flaky salt Jalapeño shaved thin, lime squeezed hard, flaky salt.

You'll need

  • 2 thick slices of sourdough
  • 4–5 ripe figs, halved
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Honey
  • Flaky salt

Plus your protein and your flavor.

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. 2–3 min a sideHeat the oil in a skillet and toast the bread, pressing it down, until golden and crisp.
  2. While it's hot, tear the burrata over it, then add any other protein.
  3. The figs, cut side up.
  4. FinishA thread of honey, your flavor, and flaky salt.

Leftovers

Toast doesn't keep. Make it fresh.
