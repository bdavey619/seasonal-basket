Charred plate

One hot pan, one protein, and whatever's charred beside it. Peppers, zucchini, a nectarine. August's ten-minute dinner.

Keep

  • A cast-iron or heavy skillet
  • Olive oil and flaky salt

August adds

Start with
  • Red bell peppers, in strips
  • Zucchini, in thick coins
Choose from
  • Nectarine halves
  • Charred pepper sauce
  • Basil
  • Burrata
  • Prosciutto

Variations

Pork chop · sweet Leftover chop, sliced and warmed, charred nectarine on the side.
Halibut · hot Seared halibut, jalapeño and lime over the top.
Burrata + prosciutto · smoky Charred vegetables around torn burrata and prosciutto, pepper sauce. Or just the burrata, for a no-meat night.

Finish

Heat the pan until it smokes. Char the vegetables first in a single layer, without moving them, until they blacken in spots. Move them to the plate, then sear or warm the protein in the same pan.

Works well with

  • Sourdough
  • Rice

From Field Notes

  • Charring a pepper
  • You don't need to salt eggplant.

House Flavor
