Pasta

Cherry tomatoes burst in olive oil until they become the sauce. Halibut flaked in, or nothing at all.

Keep

  • Pasta
  • Olive oil and garlic
  • Salt

August adds

Start with
  • Cherry tomatoes, left whole
Choose from
  • Basil
  • Halibut
  • Jalapeño, sliced thin
  • Charred pepper sauce

Variations

Halibut · fresh Seared halibut flaked in at the end, basil.
No meat · fresh The default. Burst tomatoes and basil, nothing else.
Halibut · hot Jalapeño and lime instead of basil.

Finish

Heat olive oil and a sliced garlic clove in a wide pan, add the whole cherry tomatoes, and cook until they split and slump, pressing a few to release their juice. Toss with the pasta and a splash of pasta water. Basil goes in after the heat is off.

Works well with

  • Burrata, torn over
  • Nothing at all

From Field Notes

  • You don't need to salt eggplant.
