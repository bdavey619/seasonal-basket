Sheet-pan dinner

Drumsticks, eggplant, peppers, and cherry tomatoes on one pan. The oven does everything, and the vegetables cook in the chicken's fat.

About an hour, most of it in the oven · Dinner tonight, two lunches

1 Pick a protein

  • Chicken drumsticks 2 lbs On the pan from the start, 40–45 minutes, turned once. Done when the skin is crisp and the juices run clear.
  • Eggs 3 No meat. Cracked into gaps on the pan for the last 6 minutes.

2 Pick a flavor

  • Smoky Charred pepper sauce + lime A spoonful of the jar, lime squeezed over. How to make the charred pepper sauce →
  • Hot Jalapeño + lime + flaky salt Jalapeño shaved thin, lime squeezed hard, flaky salt.
  • Fresh Burst tomatoes + basil Cherry tomatoes split in hot olive oil, basil torn in off the heat.

You'll need

  • 1 large eggplant, in big chunks
  • 2 red bell peppers, in strips
  • 1 pint cherry tomatoes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt

Plus your protein and your flavor.

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. 425°FHeat the oven.
  2. Toss the vegetables and the salted drumsticks with the olive oil. Spread everything on a sheet pan with room between the pieces.
  3. 20 minRoast, then turn the drumsticks.
  4. 20–25 min moreRoast until the skin is crisp and the eggplant has collapsed. Eggs: crack them into gaps on the pan for the last 6 minutes.
  5. FinishYour flavor at the table.

Leftovers

Pull the leftover meat off the bone while it's warm. It's two rice bowls.
