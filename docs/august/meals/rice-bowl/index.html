Rice bowl

August turns a rice bowl into something you'd actually plan for.

Keep

  • Sticky white rice
  • Protein of choice (chicken, salmon, or nothing)

August adds

Start with
  • Roasted or sautéed bell pepper strips
  • Cherry tomatoes, halved
Choose from
  • Zucchini, quickly sautéed
  • A spoonful of charred pepper sauce
  • Fresh basil, torn

Variations

Peppers + cherry tomatoes + basil The version you'll make most often.
Peppers + zucchini + charred pepper sauce If you made the house flavor. Worth it.
Cherry tomatoes + jalapeño + cotija If you want something closer to the taco bowl direction.

Finish

Warm the bowl before assembling if you have a minute. Tear basil over the top after plating — don't add it while anything is still hot.

Works well with

  • Chicken thighs
  • Salmon
  • A soft-cooked egg
  • No added protein

From Field Notes

  • Charring a pepper

House Flavor

  • Charred Pepper Sauce
