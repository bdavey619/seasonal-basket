Rice bowl

Rice, a protein, and the month's peppers and zucchini. Where the week's leftovers go for lunch.

Keep

  • Rice
  • Olive oil and salt

August adds

Start with
  • Red bell pepper strips, roasted or raw
  • Cherry tomatoes, halved
Choose from
  • Zucchini, quickly sautéed
  • Charred pepper sauce
  • Basil
  • Jalapeño

Variations

Drumsticks · smoky Leftover meat pulled from the bone, pepper sauce.
Halibut · fresh Seared halibut, burst tomatoes, basil.
Pork chop · hot Sliced leftover chop, jalapeño, lime.

Finish

A generous spoonful of the pepper sauce on top, left to pool rather than mixed in. Basil and lime at the end.

Works well with

  • A fried egg
  • Greek yogurt, salted
