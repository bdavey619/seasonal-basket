Rice bowl

Rice, a protein, and the month's peppers and zucchini. Where the week's leftovers go for lunch.

10 minutes with cooked rice · Dinner or lunch for one

1 Pick a protein

  • Chicken drumsticks, leftover Meat from 2–3 Pulled from the bone, warmed in a pan, 2 minutes.
  • Halibut ½ lb Seared 3–4 minutes, flipped, 1–2 more.
  • Pork chop, leftover 1, sliced Warmed in a hot pan, 1 minute.

2 Pick a flavor

  • Smoky Charred pepper sauce + lime A spoonful of the jar, lime squeezed over. How to make the charred pepper sauce →
  • Fresh Burst tomatoes + basil Cherry tomatoes split in hot olive oil, basil torn in off the heat.
  • Hot Jalapeño + lime + flaky salt Jalapeño shaved thin, lime squeezed hard, flaky salt.

You'll need

  • About 1½ cups cooked rice
  • 1 red bell pepper, in strips
  • ½ zucchini, in half-moons
  • A handful of cherry tomatoes, halved
  • Basil
  • Olive oil and salt

Plus your protein and your flavor.

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. If neededCook the rice if you need to.
  2. 4–5 minSauté the pepper and zucchini in a hot pan with oil until golden at the edges. Salt them.
  3. Warm or cook your protein.
  4. Rice, vegetables, protein, tomatoes.
  5. FinishYour flavor on top, basil torn over.

Leftovers

Pack it as tomorrow's lunch, with the flavor on the side.
