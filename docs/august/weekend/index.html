The weekend meal · August

Fig, prosciutto, burrata, and arugula.

One meal should feel like the reason you went shopping. In August, this is it.

Use

  • 6–8 ripe figs, halved
  • 1 ball burrata
  • 4 slices prosciutto
  • A handful of arugula
  • Olive oil
  • Flaky salt and black pepper
  • Optional: a drizzle of honey or aged balsamic

If figs aren't at their peak, nectarines work. The dish follows the same logic — ripe fruit, good cheese, salt, oil.

Do

Arrange figs, torn burrata, and prosciutto on a plate. Scatter arugula. Drizzle with olive oil, flaky salt, black pepper. Add honey or balsamic if you have it. Eat with bread.
