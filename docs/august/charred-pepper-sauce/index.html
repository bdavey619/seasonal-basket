Charred Pepper Sauce

One jar. A week of flavor.

Make one jar

  • 3–4 red bell peppers
  • 1–2 jalapeños
  • 1 garlic clove, unpeeled
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp sherry vinegar
  • Flaky salt

Set a dry cast-iron pan over high heat until smoking. Add the peppers and jalapeños whole, and the garlic clove. Leave them alone — dark spots are the point. Turn occasionally until charred on most sides, about 12–15 minutes. Transfer peppers and jalapeños to a bowl, cover tight, and leave to steam for 15 minutes. Peel off skins and discard seeds. Squeeze the garlic from its skin. Blend everything with olive oil, sherry vinegar, and salt — leave a little texture rather than making it perfectly smooth. Taste and adjust.

Keeps in the refrigerator for up to a week. The flavor deepens overnight.

Use it this week

Rice bowl Spoon it.

A generous spoonful on top. Let it pool slightly rather than mixing it in.

Chicken thighs Serve alongside.

Spoon it next to the chicken rather than over it. Let the diner decide how much.

Eggs Stir it in.

Add a spoonful to scrambled eggs in the last 30 seconds. Or spoon it over fried eggs.

Pasta Thin it.

Stir in a little olive oil or pasta water. Toss with warm pasta off the heat.

White beans Let it sit.

Spoon over beans and give them a few minutes. They absorb it slowly.
