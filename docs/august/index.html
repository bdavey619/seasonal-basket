Proteins

Thick pork chops 2, bone-in, 1¼ inches Pork loves stone fruit: the sweetness and acid of a charred nectarine cut through the fat at the bone. Salted early, it stays juicy. The weekend meal, the charred plate, fig toast
Halibut 1 lb Lean, delicate fish wants something juicy and acidic cooked around it — burst cherry tomatoes, not a heavy sauce. Pasta, a rice bowl, the charred plate
Chicken drumsticks 2 lbs All dark meat and skin, drumsticks can take the hottest, longest roast — and the peppers and eggplant collapse in their fat. The sheet pan, a rice bowl, lunches

One thing to notice

The color of a ripe fig.

A ripe fig darkens all the way to deep purple-black and sometimes splits at the base. Look for a small bead of sweetness near the stem. That's the one to buy.
