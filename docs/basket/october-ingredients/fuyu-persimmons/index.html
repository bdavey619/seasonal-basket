October · San DiegoIf you see it

Fuyu persimmons

Fuyus come from North County groves, usually in the last weeks of the month — so don't go looking on the first. When they turn up, they're one of the best things in the store, and almost nobody knows what to do with them.

The default move

Eat it firm, like an apple.

Slice crosswise → no need to peel → salt + olive oil, or cheese

How to choose it

  • Squat and flat on top, shaped like a tomato — that's a Fuyu
  • Deep orange, firm but not rock hard
  • Tall and acorn-shaped is a Hachiya: leave it until it's soft as jelly

Buy this much: 3–4, when you see them.

During the week

  • Sliced into rounds with pomegranate and a little salt
  • With cheese, instead of an apple
  • Alongside pork chops
  • Diced over yogurt

One thing worth learning

The two persimmons in the bin are not interchangeable. A firm Hachiya is so tannic it dries out your whole mouth; it has to go completely soft first. A Fuyu has no such problem and is good crisp. Learn the shape and you'll never make the mistake.

Good with this month

Pomegranates, Late Julian apples

Good with your staples

Greek yogurt, sourdough

Storage

On the counter until they give very slightly, then the fridge for a week or two.
