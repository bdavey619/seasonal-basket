October · San Diego

Pomegranates

California grows nearly all of the country's pomegranates, and the harvest runs from late September into November. In October they're heavy, cheap by the piece, and in every produce section. By spring, the only ones left are imported.

How to choose it

  • Heavy for its size — weight is juice
  • Angular and a little lopsided, with flat faces; round ones aren't full yet
  • Deep red, leathery skin
  • A small dry crack is fine; a soft or damp spot is not

Buy this much: 3. One for the drink, two for scattering. One pomegranate gives about a cup of seeds.

Priced by the piece, so the heaviest one in the bin is the best deal.

During the week

  • Over the warm plate
  • On fish tacos, in place of salsa
  • Over a baked sweet potato
  • On Greek yogurt with chopped dates, for breakfast

On a weekend

Half a pomegranate's seeds in the October salad, beside the seared fish.

One thing worth learning

To seed one without a mess, score around the crown, pull the fruit apart into sections, and work the seeds loose in a bowl of water. The seeds sink and the white pith floats. Skim, drain, done.

Good with this month

Kabocha squash, Sweet potatoes, Arugula, Medjool dates, Rockfish fillets

Good with your staples

Greek yogurt, rice, black beans

Storage

Whole, in the fridge for a month or more. Seeded, five days in a covered container.
