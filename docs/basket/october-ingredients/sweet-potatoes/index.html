October · San Diego

Sweet potatoes

Most of California's sweet potatoes come out of the ground around Merced in September and October. The new crop is on the shelves now — at its cheapest, and not yet tired from months in storage.

How to choose it

  • Firm all over, with no soft ends
  • No sprouts and no wrinkling
  • Medium and even in shape, so they cook at one speed
  • Orange-fleshed (garnet or jewel) for roasting

Buy this much: 2 lbs. They keep, so they're the thing you cook the week it gets away from you.

Cheapest of the year through November, when the holidays push the price back up.

During the week

  • Cubed and roasted into tacos with poblano
  • In a rice bowl with ground beef and pomegranate
  • Split open with yogurt, salt, and salsa macha
  • Cut side down beside pork chops

On a weekend

A whole tray, halved and roasted cut side down, on the table next to the braise.

One thing worth learning

Cut side down is the whole trick. The flat face sits on the hot pan, the sugars caramelize into a dark crust, and the inside steams in its own skin. Flip it halfway and you lose the crust. Leave it alone for thirty-five minutes.

Good with this month

The last poblanos, Pomegranates, Late Julian apples

Good with your staples

ground beef or turkey, Greek yogurt, beans, sticky white rice

Storage

Somewhere cool and dark, not the fridge — cold turns the center hard and odd-tasting. They keep for weeks.
