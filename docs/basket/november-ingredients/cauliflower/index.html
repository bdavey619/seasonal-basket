Cauliflower

Cauliflower is a cool-season crop, and it's sweeter and denser now than at any time in summer. Roasted hard, it turns nutty and golden at the edges.

How to choose it

  • Tight, creamy-white curds with no brown spots
  • Heavy for its size
  • Fresh, green leaves hugging the head

Buy this much: 1 head for the salmon and lentils.

Sold by the head. A heavier head for the same price is more cauliflower.

During the week

  • Roasted and folded into warm lentils under salmon
  • Florets in the one-pot chicken or the minestrone, if you have extra

On a weekend

Leftover florets, crisped in a skillet, under a fried egg.

One thing worth learning

Cauliflower is mostly water, so it browns only when it has room. Spread it out on the pan with space around each piece; crowded, it steams and stays pale.

Good with this month

Fennel, Satsuma mandarins

Good with your staples

chicken thighs, eggs

Storage

In the fridge, unwashed, in a loose bag, for up to a week.
