Leeks

Leeks come into their own as the weather cools. Cooked slowly in butter, they melt into something soft and sweet that turns a braise, a soup, or a bowl of pasta into a cool-weather dish.

How to choose it

  • Straight, firm white and light-green parts
  • Fresh, dark tops without yellowing
  • Medium leeks are more tender than very thick ones

Buy this much: 2 for each leek dish you pick.

Sold singly or in bunches of two or three.

During the week

  • Melted through orecchiette with chard
  • The base of the beef and white bean soup
  • Left over on toast with an egg

On a weekend

Softened with fennel under the braised chicken thighs.

One thing worth learning

Leeks hold grit between their layers. Slice them first, then swish the slices in a bowl of cold water and lift them out with your hands; the sand sinks to the bottom.

Good with this month

Fennel, Swiss chard

Good with your staples

chicken thighs, eggs

Storage

In the fridge, unwashed, for up to two weeks.
