Hot-pan dinner

One very hot pan: sear the protein, then char corn and peaches in what's left. Ten minutes, and the pan does the cooking.

15 minutes · Dinner for one

1 Pick a protein

  • Skirt steak ¾ lb Seared first, 2–3 minutes a side, then rested while the corn and peaches char.
  • Shrimp ½ lb Seared first, 1–2 minutes a side.

2 Pick a flavor

  • Smoky Charred tomato salsa + lime A spoonful of the salsa, lime squeezed over. How to make the charred tomato salsa →
  • Sweet and sharp Peach + lime + flaky salt Sliced ripe peach, lime, salt. A few rings of jalapeño if you want heat.
  • Bright Tomato vinaigrette + basil Spoon the jar over, tear basil on top, flaky salt last. How to make the tomato herb vinaigrette →

You'll need

  • 2 ears of corn, kernels cut off
  • 2 peaches, halved and pitted
  • Olive oil
  • Flaky salt
  • A few basil leaves

Plus your protein and your flavor.

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. HighHeat a cast-iron or heavy skillet until it smokes.
  2. SearSalt your protein and sear it with a little oil — don't move it. Steak: 2–3 minutes a side. Shrimp: 1–2. Move it to a plate; steak rests.
  3. 2–3 minPeach halves into the same pan, cut side down, until they char.
  4. 3 minCorn kernels in next, until they blacken in spots. Salt them.
  5. Everything on one plate, with the juices from the resting meat. Slice the steak across the grain.
  6. FinishYour flavor, basil torn over, flaky salt.

Leftovers

Leftover steak or shrimp is tomorrow's tacos or rice bowl.
