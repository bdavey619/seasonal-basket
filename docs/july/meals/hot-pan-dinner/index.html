Hot-pan dinner

One very hot pan: sear the protein, then char corn and peaches in what's left. Ten minutes, and the pan does the cooking.

Keep

  • A cast-iron or heavy skillet
  • Olive oil and flaky salt

July adds

Start with
  • Sweet corn, cut into short lengths or kernels
  • Peaches, halved
Choose from
  • Limes
  • Basil
  • Charred tomato salsa

Variations

Skirt steak · smoky Steak first, rested while the corn and peaches char. Salsa at the table.
Shrimp · sweet and sharp Shrimp for two minutes, then the peaches. Lime over everything.
Shrimp · bright Basil and a spoonful of vinaigrette over the shrimp and corn.

Finish

Heat the pan until it smokes. Sear the protein first and set it aside. Then the corn and peach halves, cut side down, until they char in spots — two or three minutes. Everything goes on one plate with the juices from the resting meat.

Works well with

  • Rice
  • Toasted sourdough

From Field Notes

  • Flaky salt goes on last

House Flavor

  • Charred Tomato Salsa
