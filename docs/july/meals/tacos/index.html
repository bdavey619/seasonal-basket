Tacos

Charred salsa, corn, and lime on warm tortillas. Skirt steak, shrimp, or beans — the tortilla doesn't mind.

20 minutes · Dinner for one, about 6 tacos

1 Pick a protein

  • Skirt steak ¾ lb Seared 2–3 minutes a side in a smoking pan, rested 5, sliced thin across the grain.
  • Shrimp ½ lb Seared 1–2 minutes a side.
  • Black beans 1 can, drained No meat. Warmed in a small pan, 5 minutes.

2 Pick a flavor

  • Smoky Charred tomato salsa + lime A spoonful of the salsa, lime squeezed over. How to make the charred tomato salsa →
  • Sweet and sharp Peach + lime + flaky salt Sliced ripe peach, lime, salt. A few rings of jalapeño if you want heat.
  • Cool Greek yogurt + cucumber + mint Grated cucumber stirred into salted yogurt, mint torn in.

You'll need

  • 12 corn tortillas — two per taco
  • 1 ear of corn, kernels cut off
  • 1 lime
  • Olive oil and salt

Plus your protein and your flavor.

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. TortillasWarm the tortillas in a dry pan until they blister in spots. Stack them under a towel.
  2. 3 minChar the corn kernels in the same dry pan until they blacken in spots. Salt them.
  3. 2–3 min a sideSteak: salt it, sear in a smoking pan with a little oil, and don't move it. Shrimp: 1–2 minutes a side. Beans: warm through.
  4. Rest 5 minSteak: rest it 5 minutes, then slice thin across the grain — that's what makes skirt steak tender.
  5. FinishTwo tortillas, protein, corn, then your flavor. Lime last.

Leftovers

Leftover steak goes into tomorrow's rice bowl.
