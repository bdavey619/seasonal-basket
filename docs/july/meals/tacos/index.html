Tacos

Charred salsa, corn, and lime on warm tortillas. Skirt steak, shrimp, or beans — the tortilla doesn't mind.

Keep

  • Corn tortillas
  • Limes

July adds

Start with
  • Charred tomato salsa
  • Sweet corn, cut from the cob
Choose from
  • Persian cucumber, diced
  • Sliced peach
  • Salted Greek yogurt

Variations

Skirt steak · smoky The default. Seared hard, sliced thin across the grain, charred salsa.
Shrimp · sweet and sharp Seared shrimp, peach and lime, a little jalapeño.
Black beans · smoky No meat. Warm beans, corn, salsa, lime.

Finish

Warm the tortillas in a dry pan until they blister in spots, two per taco. Sear the steak in a smoking-hot pan, two or three minutes a side, rest it five, and slice across the grain — that's what makes skirt steak tender.

Works well with

  • Black beans
  • Rice
