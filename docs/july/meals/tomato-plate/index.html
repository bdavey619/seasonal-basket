July · weekday

Tomato plate

Thick slices of the best tomatoes of the year, something cool beside them, and a protein on top. The meal July does best.

Keep

  • Olive oil
  • Flaky salt and black pepper

July adds

Start with
  • Ripe tomatoes, sliced thick
  • Persian cucumbers
Choose from
  • Basil and mint
  • Tomato herb vinaigrette
  • Salted Greek yogurt
  • Sliced peach

Variations

Wild salmon · cool Leftover salmon in big flakes, cucumber yogurt, mint.
Shrimp · bright Quickly seared shrimp, vinaigrette, basil.
Burrata · bright No meat. Torn burrata, vinaigrette, basil, sourdough.

Finish

Salt the tomatoes first and let them sit while you get everything else ready — five minutes draws out their juice, which becomes the dressing. Flaky salt again at the very end.

Works well with

  • Toasted sourdough
  • Black beans

From Field Notes

  • Flaky salt goes on last
  • Add herbs after the heat
