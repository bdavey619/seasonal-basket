Rice bowl

Rice, a protein, and the cool, crunchy things July does well. The bowl that carries leftovers into lunch.

Keep

  • Rice
  • Olive oil and flaky salt

July adds

Start with
  • Persian cucumber, sliced thin
  • Ripe tomato, chopped
Choose from
  • Sweet corn
  • Mint and basil
  • Tomato herb vinaigrette
  • Salted Greek yogurt

Variations

Wild salmon · cool Flaked salmon, cucumber yogurt, mint.
Skirt steak · bright Sliced leftover steak, tomato, vinaigrette.
Skirt steak · smoky Charred salsa and corn instead.

Finish

Let the rice cool a little before building the bowl — July food is better warm than hot. Herbs go on last, after everything else.

Works well with

  • A fried egg
  • Black beans

From Field Notes

  • Add herbs after the heat
