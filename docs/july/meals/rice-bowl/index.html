Rice bowl

Rice, a protein, and the cool, crunchy things July does well. The bowl that carries leftovers into lunch.

10 minutes with cooked rice · Dinner or lunch for one

1 Pick a protein

  • Wild salmon, leftover A big piece Flaked, cold or barely warmed.
  • Skirt steak, leftover A few slices Warmed in a hot pan, 1 minute.
  • Shrimp ½ lb Seared 1–2 minutes a side.

2 Pick a flavor

  • Cool Greek yogurt + cucumber + mint Grated cucumber stirred into salted yogurt, mint torn in.
  • Bright Tomato vinaigrette + basil Spoon the jar over, tear basil on top, flaky salt last. How to make the tomato herb vinaigrette →
  • Smoky Charred tomato salsa + lime A spoonful of the salsa, lime squeezed over. How to make the charred tomato salsa →

You'll need

  • About 1½ cups cooked rice
  • 1 Persian cucumber, sliced thin
  • 1 ripe tomato, chopped
  • Kernels from ½ ear of corn, raw
  • Mint and basil
  • Salt

Plus your protein and your flavor.

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. If neededCook the rice if you need to, and let it cool a little — July food is better warm than hot.
  2. Warm or cook your protein.
  3. Rice in the bowl, then the cucumber, tomato, and corn, then the protein.
  4. FinishYour flavor on top.
  5. Herbs last, torn over everything.

Leftovers

Pack it as tomorrow's lunch, with the flavor in a separate container.
