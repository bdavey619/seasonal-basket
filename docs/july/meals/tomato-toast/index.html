Tomato toast

Thick sourdough, a ripe tomato, olive oil, salt. In July, that's dinner — add an egg if you want it to be.

10 minutes · Dinner for one

1 Pick a protein

  • Fried eggs 2 Cooked in the same pan after the toast, about 3 minutes.
  • Burrata ½ ball Torn over the hot toast. Nothing to cook.

2 Pick a flavor

  • Bright Tomato vinaigrette + basil Spoon the jar over, tear basil on top, flaky salt last. How to make the tomato herb vinaigrette →
  • Smoky Charred tomato salsa + lime A spoonful of the salsa, lime squeezed over. How to make the charred tomato salsa →
  • Cool Greek yogurt + cucumber + mint Grated cucumber stirred into salted yogurt, mint torn in.

You'll need

  • 2 thick slices of sourdough
  • 1–2 ripe tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove, halved
  • Flaky salt

Plus your protein and your flavor.

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. 2–3 min a sideHeat the oil in a skillet and toast the bread, pressing it down, until golden and crisp.
  2. Rub the cut garlic over the hot toast.
  3. 3 minEggs: crack them into the same pan and cook until the whites set. Burrata: tear it over the toast now.
  4. Slice the tomato, lay it on, and salt it.
  5. FinishYour flavor on top, then the egg if you made one.

Leftovers

Toast doesn't keep. Make it fresh.
