Tomato toast

Thick sourdough, a ripe tomato, olive oil, salt. In July, that's dinner — add an egg if you want it to be.

Keep

  • Sourdough, thick slices
  • Olive oil and flaky salt

July adds

Start with
  • Ripe tomatoes
Choose from
  • A fried egg
  • Burrata
  • Basil
  • Tomato herb vinaigrette

Variations

Fried egg · bright The default. Tomato, egg, a spoonful of vinaigrette.
Burrata · bright Torn burrata under the tomato, basil over it.
Fried egg · smoky Charred salsa instead of fresh tomato.

Finish

Toast the bread in a skillet with olive oil until it's crisp. Rub the cut side of a garlic clove over it while it's hot, then pile on the tomato, salt it, and eat it standing up if you want.

Works well with

  • A cucumber and mint salad
  • A peach, after

From Field Notes

  • Flaky salt goes on last
