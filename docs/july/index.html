Proteins

Wild salmon 1½ lbs Rich, oily fish wants something cool and sharp against it — cucumber, mint, a raw tomato — not something heavier. The weekend meal, the tomato plate, a rice bowl
Skirt steak 1½ lbs Beefy, charred meat wants acid and freshness at the end. Charred salsa and a squeeze of lime do what a steak sauce would, lighter. Tacos, the hot-pan dinner, lunches
Shrimp 1 lb, frozen is fine Shrimp is sweet and cooks in two minutes, so it wants something sweeter and something sharp beside it — corn and peach, with lime. The hot-pan dinner, tacos, the tomato plate

Also buy 3 limes, 1 white onion, 1 jalapeño, corn tortillas, 1 ball burrata, sherry vinegar, tart cherry juice, sparkling water

From your kitchen rice, black beans, sourdough, Greek yogurt, eggs, olive oil, garlic, honey, flaky salt, black pepper

July's four flavors
  • Bright Tomato vinaigrette + basil Spoon the jar over, tear basil on top, flaky salt last.
  • Smoky Charred tomato salsa + lime A spoonful of the salsa, lime squeezed over.
  • Cool Greek yogurt + cucumber + mint Grated cucumber stirred into salted yogurt, mint torn in.
  • Sweet and sharp Peach + lime + flaky salt Sliced ripe peach, lime, salt. A few rings of jalapeño if you want heat.
