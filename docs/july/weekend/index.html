The weekend meal · July

Tomato, burrata, basil, and sourdough.

One meal should feel like the reason you went shopping. This is July’s.

Use

  • 2 ripe tomatoes
  • 1 ball burrata or fresh mozzarella
  • Basil
  • Olive oil
  • Flaky salt and black pepper
  • Toasted sourdough

Burrata is richer and more indulgent. Fresh mozzarella is a reliable everyday alternative at about half the price — Trader Joe’s and Whole Foods both carry it.

Do

Slice the tomatoes thickly. Tear open the cheese. Add basil, olive oil, salt, and pepper. Use warm sourdough to drag through everything left on the plate.
