Chicken thighs and cauliflower

Bone-in thighs roasted hot until the skin crackles, cauliflower browning in the fat around them, and the month's jar spooned over everything — brine and all — so the pan juices become a sharp, bright sauce.

About 45 minutes, most of it in the oven · Dinner tonight and a lunch

About 1,000 kcal and 60 g protein a serving

You'll need

  • 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 1¾ lbs)
  • 1 head cauliflower, in large florets
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • A satsuma, peeled and segmented
  • Pickled fennel, a big forkful, plus 2 tbsp of its brine
  • Salt, black pepper, and chile flakes
  • Rice: about 1 cup cooked per serving

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. 450°FHeat the oven. Pat the chicken dry and salt it on both sides.
  2. 15 minSet the thighs skin-side up on a sheet pan, spread out, and roast them on their own.
  3. Toss the cauliflower with the olive oil, salt, pepper, and a pinch of chile flakes.
  4. 20–25 minScatter the cauliflower around the chicken in one layer, with room between the pieces, and roast until the skin is crisp, the cauliflower is browned underneath, and the thickest thigh reads about 175°F.
  5. FinishSpoon the pickled fennel and its brine over the hot pan, scatter the satsuma segments, and scrape everything together with the juices. Over rice.

Leftovers

Pack the second serving before you sit down: chicken, cauliflower, and rice, with a little more pickled fennel. Good warm or at room temperature.

How it works

Thighs get a head start because they take longer than the cauliflower; by the time the florets go in, the pan is slick with chicken fat, and the cauliflower roasts in it. Hot oven, room on the pan, and no stirring is what browns it. The brine at the end does what a squeeze of lemon would, and the satsuma adds sweetness to the sharpness.

Make it your own

  • Fennel wedges in place of half the cauliflower.
  • A spoonful of melted leeks under the chicken before serving.
  • Grated Parmesan over the cauliflower as it comes out.

Checked against

  • Roasted Chicken Thighs with Cauliflower and Pomegranate Pan Sauce — America's Test Kitchen — 450°F; chicken and cauliflower on one pan, thighs to 175°F
  • Sheet-Pan Chicken and Cauliflower — Food52 — thighs alone for 15 minutes, then cauliflower around them for about 15 more
