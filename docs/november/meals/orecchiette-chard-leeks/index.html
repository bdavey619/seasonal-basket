Orecchiette with chard and leeks

Leeks cooked slowly in butter until they melt, chard stems and leaves folded in, and little ear-shaped pasta catching all of it, with chile and plenty of Parmesan.

30 minutes · Dinner tonight and a lunch

About 800 kcal and 30 g protein a serving

You'll need

  • 8 oz orecchiette
  • 2 leeks, sliced thin and rinsed
  • 1 bunch Swiss chard, stems chopped, leaves torn
  • 2 tbsp butter and 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 oz Parmesan, grated
  • ½ lemon
  • Salt, black pepper, and chile flakes

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. 10–12 minMelt the butter with the oil in a large skillet over medium-low. Add the leeks and a pinch of salt, cover, and cook, stirring now and then, until they're very soft and sweet but not browned.
  2. Bring a big pot of well-salted water to a boil.
  3. 5 minUncover the leeks, add the chard stems, garlic, and a good pinch of chile flakes, and cook until the stems are tender.
  4. About 11 minBoil the orecchiette until just tender. Before you drain it, scoop out a cup of the pasta water.
  5. 2 minAdd the chard leaves to the skillet and stir until wilted. Add the pasta, half the Parmesan, and a splash of pasta water, and toss over the heat until it turns glossy and clings.
  6. FinishThe rest of the Parmesan, black pepper, and a squeeze of lemon.

Leftovers

Pack a bowl for lunch. It's best warmed in a skillet with a splash of water, or at room temperature with more lemon.

Make it a meal

Fold a can of drained white beans in with the chard leaves. They soak up the leeks and Parmesan and make it a heartier bowl.

How it works

Leeks cooked low and covered don't brown; they melt into a soft, sweet sauce of their own, which is why this needs so little else. Starchy pasta water and Parmesan, tossed over the heat, pull it all into something creamy without any cream. Chard stems go in before the leaves because they need longer.

Make it your own

  • Any short pasta that catches sauce: rigatoni, shells, or penne.
  • Shredded leftover braised chicken folded in at the end.
  • Cauliflower florets boiled with the pasta, in place of the chard.
