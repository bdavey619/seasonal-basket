Salmon over warm lentils

A French classic, made with November's jar: French green lentils dressed warm with the pickled fennel's brine, dark-roasted cauliflower folded through, and salmon roasted on the same pan, set on top.

45 minutes · Dinner tonight and a lunch

About 1,000 kcal and 75 g protein a serving, depending on the fish

You'll need

  • 1 lb salmon fillet, about 1 inch thick, in two pieces
  • ¾ cup French green lentils, rinsed
  • 1 head cauliflower, in small florets
  • Pickled fennel, a big forkful, plus 3 tbsp of its brine
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • Grated Parmesan
  • Salt, black pepper, and chile flakes

Make it

Tap a step when it's done.

  1. 425°F · 30 minHeat the oven. Toss the cauliflower with 2 tbsp oil and salt, spread it out on a sheet pan, and roast, stirring once, until golden at the edges.
  2. 20–25 minMeanwhile, cover the lentils with a few inches of water in a pot, add a pinch of salt, and simmer until tender but still holding their shape. Drain.
  3. While they're warm, toss the lentils with the brine, 2 tbsp olive oil, the garlic, salt, pepper, and a pinch of chile flakes.
  4. 10–12 minFor the cauliflower's last stretch, push it to the sides, salt the salmon, and set it skin-side down in the middle of the pan. Roast until it flakes at the thickest part (the USDA's safe temperature is 145°F). Salmon goes from silky to dry quickly, so check at 10.
  5. FinishFold the cauliflower and pickled fennel into the lentils, set the salmon on top, and finish with Parmesan and black pepper.

Leftovers

Keep the second piece of salmon and a bowl of lentils for lunch. Eat them at room temperature with a squeeze of lemon; don't reheat the fish.

How it works

Salmon over lentils is a French bistro dish for a reason: rich, oily fish wants something earthy and sharp underneath it. Lentils take in a dressing best while they're warm, and the jar's brine is the vinegar. French green lentils hold their shape where brown ones turn soft.

Make it your own

  • Two fried eggs instead of the salmon, for a meat-free night.
  • Halibut instead of salmon; it needs a few minutes less.
  • Melted leeks folded in with the cauliflower.
