The drink · November

Sherman Heights Café de Olla

One drink. The evenings turned, and November opens with Día de los Muertos.

Keep

  • Ground coffee
  • Water

November adds

  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • ¼ cup dark brown sugar
  • A strip of satsuma peel

Method

Bring four cups of water to a simmer in a saucepan with the cinnamon stick, brown sugar, and satsuma peel, and let it bubble gently for five minutes so the cinnamon opens up. Take it off the heat, stir in a third of a cup of ground coffee, and let it steep for four minutes. Pour it through a fine strainer, or a paper filter if you want it clean. It should taste like coffee first, then spice, and only a little sweet. It's traditionally made in a clay pot, the olla; any saucepan works.

Try another way

Less sugar Start with two tablespoons if you take your coffee black.
Stronger A heaped half cup of coffee instead of a third, for slow mornings.
Lemon peel instead of satsuma Sharper and less floral.
Iced For the warm afternoons November still has.
