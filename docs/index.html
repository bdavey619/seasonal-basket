Cook with the year.

The grocery store offers everything, all the time. Each month, Seasonal narrows that down — the handful of ingredients worth buying while they're at their best, one flavor that works across the week, one drink that captures the season.

Your cooking stays exactly as it is. It starts to taste like the month.

November
San Diego

November is when San Diego's evenings finally turn. The days still reach the high sixties, but the nights drop into the fifties, and for the first time since spring an hour with a pot in the oven sounds good. The first satsumas arrive, persimmons ripen on bare trees, and the cool-weather vegetables come back — leeks, fennel, chard, cauliflower. Everything below comes from one store and a fish market, and it all keeps you cooking through the week.
